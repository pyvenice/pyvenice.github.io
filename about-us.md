## Venice Python User Group

[PyVenice](https://www.meetup.com/pyvenice/) is a meetup community for anyone interested in Python, at all levels, from complete beginners to seasoned professionals, dedicated to promoting Python throughout the Veneto region: it has 546 members and 8 events.

## How we organize our meetups

PyVenice is a travelling group: in 2026 we met in Mestre, Padua, Verona, Treviso and Vicenza. The constant is that every meetup is always also in streaming, then saved on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@{{ site.youtube_channel }}), and in person, like every meetup, it ends with networking, having a drink and a bite together.
Each meetup has two talks. Talks are technical: no marketing, except to name your own company or product.

We talk about software development, cloud-native technologies and software engineering; libraries and packaging, from Python and CPython to the integration with Rust; frontend, backend, frameworks and full-stack development; research and applied Python projects; TDD, CI/CD and secure software development; AI coding assistants and developer tools.

Do you want to give a talk? Send your proposal on [Sessionize](https://sessionize.com/{{ site.sessionize_page }}).

## Other initiatives

Besides the meetups, we organize workshops on software engineering best practices in Python development and on the use of AI assistants.

## Venice Python User Group

[PyVenice](https://www.meetup.com/pyvenice/) è una community di meetup per chiunque sia interessato a Python, a tutti i livelli, dai principianti ai professionisti esperti, con l'obiettivo di promuovere Python in tutto il Veneto: conta 546 membri e 8 eventi.

## Come organizziamo i meetup

PyVenice è un gruppo itinerante: nel 2026 siamo stati a Mestre, Padova, Verona, Treviso e Vicenza. La costante è che ogni meetup è sempre anche in streaming, poi salvato su [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@{{ site.youtube_channel }}), e in presenza, come ogni meetup, finisce in networking, a bere e mangiare qualcosa insieme.
Ogni meetup prevede due talk. I talk sono tecnici: niente marketing, se non per nominare la propria azienda o il proprio prodotto.

Parliamo di sviluppo software, tecnologie cloud-native e software engineering; librerie e packaging, da Python e CPython all'integrazione con Rust; frontend, backend, framework e sviluppo full-stack; ricerca e progetti Python applicati; TDD, CI/CD e sviluppo software sicuro; AI coding assistant e strumenti per sviluppatori.
